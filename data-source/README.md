# data-source

Raw upstream word lists. Nothing here is loaded by the game; it is the input for building packs in `src/data/packs/`.

## cefrj-vocabulary-profile-1.5.csv

The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies (Tono Laboratory, TUFS).
Source: https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj

Terms as stated by the publisher: the CEFR-J vocabulary and grammar profile datasets can be used for research and
commercial purposes with no charge, provided the dataset is cited properly. The copyright belongs to Tono Laboratory
at TUFS. Keep this citation wherever the data (or a pack derived from it) is shipped.
